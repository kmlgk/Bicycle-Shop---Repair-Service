Our Story

A one-stand repair garage that grew into Ashbrook's neighbourhood bicycle shop — meet the people behind every ticket.

Since 2014

Started on a single repair stand in a rented garage

Rosa Tovar opened Spoke & Gear with one repair stand, a toolbox, and a promise: tell customers exactly what their bike needs, and nothing it doesn't. Word got around Ashbrook fast.

By 2017 the garage had become a proper storefront on Millrace Lane, with room to sell road, mountain and kids' bikes alongside a growing repair workshop. But the one thing that never scaled well was communication — customers still had to call in just to ask "is it ready yet?"

In 2023 we built a customer dashboard so anyone could check a ticket's status from their phone — in queue, being serviced, or ready for pickup — plus every past receipt, without ever picking up the phone.
