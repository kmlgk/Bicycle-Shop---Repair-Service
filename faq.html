Repairs & Live Tracking

How your dashboard and ticket system works.

Every bike checked in gets a ticket number. Sign in to your dashboard any time to see whether it's In Queue, Being Serviced, or Ready for Pickup, along with an estimated pickup time.

Yes — you'll get an email the moment your ticket moves to "Ready for Pickup," and your dashboard updates instantly.

Walk-ins are welcome for quick fixes like flats. For tune-ups, suspension work or overhauls, use the booking form on our Services page to reserve a drop-off slot.

Every completed repair — with full cost breakdown and mechanic notes — stays in your dashboard's history tab indefinitely, handy for warranty claims or resale.
